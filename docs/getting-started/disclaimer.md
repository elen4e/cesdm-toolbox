## CESDM Demonstration Release

The **Common Energy System Domain Model (CESDM)** is an open initiative
to establish a common, schema-driven representation of energy systems
that can be shared across modelling, simulation, optimisation, planning,
and operational tools.

This release presents the current state of the CESDM methodology
together with its accompanying toolbox. It demonstrates the modelling
concepts, software architecture, and workflows that form the foundation
of CESDM.

While CESDM will continue to evolve, the concepts, schemas, and toolbox
provided in this release already offer a practical basis for developing
reusable, interoperable, and well-structured energy system models.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Project Context

The development of CESDM is carried out within the framework of the
**SWEET-CoSi project** (**SWEET -- Co-evolution of the Swiss Energy
System and Society**).

- **SWEET-CoSi:** [Project homepage](https://sweet-cosi.ch)
- **SWEET Programme:** [Swiss Federal Office of Energy (SFOE)](https://www.bfe.admin.ch/bfe/en/home/research-and-cleantech/funding-program-sweet.html)

Within SWEET-CoSi, CESDM is being developed as part of **Task 1.9**,
which focuses on:

-   conceptual and methodological foundations for common energy system
    representations;
-   shared domain models supporting interoperability across
    energy-system tools and research tasks.

The CESDM toolbox presented here serves as the reference implementation
of these concepts.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Objectives of this Release

The objectives of this release are to:

-   demonstrate the CESDM modelling methodology;
-   illustrate the schema-driven design principles;
-   showcase the capabilities of the CESDM toolbox;
-   provide practical examples for learning and experimentation;
-   encourage discussion, feedback, and community collaboration;
-   establish a common foundation for future interoperability across
    energy-system tools.

This release is intentionally designed to be accessible while already
demonstrating the core modelling principles of CESDM.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Current Development Status

CESDM is an actively evolving project.

The schemas included in this release represent the current
methodological foundation and are expected to evolve as additional use
cases, experience, and community feedback are incorporated.

Future versions will continue to improve:

-   semantic consistency;
-   ontology alignment;
-   validation capabilities;
-   schema completeness;
-   interoperability between analysis tools;
-   long-term stability and compatibility.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## What This Release Is

This release provides:

-   a practical implementation of the CESDM methodology;
-   a schema-driven modelling framework;
-   a toolbox for creating, validating, and exchanging CESDM models;
-   reference examples illustrating recommended modelling practices;
-   a foundation for further collaboration and future standardisation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Intended Audience

CESDM is intended for:

-   researchers developing energy-system models;
-   software developers working on model interoperability;
-   practitioners integrating multiple analysis tools;
-   organisations interested in common data models;
-   contributors who would like to participate in the future development
    of CESDM.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Community and Collaboration

CESDM is intended to evolve collaboratively with the energy modelling
community.

Feedback from researchers, software developers, infrastructure
operators, and other stakeholders is highly appreciated. Practical
experience, new modelling requirements, and additional use cases will
directly influence future versions of the schemas, toolbox, and
documentation.

Our long-term goal is to establish CESDM as a widely adopted Common
Energy System Domain Model that promotes interoperability, transparency,
reuse, and collaboration across the energy modelling ecosystem.

We warmly welcome discussions, suggestions, and contributions from the
community.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Looking Ahead

Future releases of CESDM will continue to expand and refine:

-   the Common Energy System Domain Ontology;
-   schema definitions and semantic consistency;
-   reusable technology libraries;
-   validation profiles;
-   interoperability with additional analysis tools;
-   documentation, examples, and modelling guidelines.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## In Summary

This release represents an important milestone towards a shared Common
Energy System Domain Model.

Rather than a finished destination, CESDM is an evolving collaborative
initiative. We invite the community to explore the methodology, use the
toolbox, provide feedback, and help shape the future development of
CESDM.
